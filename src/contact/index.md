<h1 class="page-title">Contact</h1>
<p class="page-intro">

  club or a group, write to
  <a href="mailto:contact@dermotcochran.com">contact@dermotcochran.com</a>.
</p>
<p>

  photograph are welcome on the page itself, where the discussion box is open.
</p>
