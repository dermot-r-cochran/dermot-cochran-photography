Two years to arrive at this. Spear thistle is a biennial: a flat rosette of
spined leaves in the first year, hugging the ground where nothing grazes it,
then the stem, the flower and the seed in the second — and then it dies. The
ladybird and the hoverfly elsewhere in this album came to the same plant, a few
frames apart.
