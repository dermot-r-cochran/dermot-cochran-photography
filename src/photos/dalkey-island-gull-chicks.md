These grow into the largest gulls in the world. It takes about four years to
reach the slate-black mantle and the bulk that lets an adult swallow a puffin
or a rabbit whole — the bird in *Great Black-backed Gull*, photographed a month
later on Ireland's Eye, is the finished article. Nothing about them at this age
suggests it.
