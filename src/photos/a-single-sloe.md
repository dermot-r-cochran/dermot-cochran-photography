Blackthorn flowers before it leafs, so its blossom comes as white froth on bare
black wood in March, weeks ahead of the hawthorn and while there is little else
out. This is what becomes of it. The pale sheen on the skin is a natural wax the
fruit puts down itself, and it rubs off at a touch, which is why sloes almost
never look like this in the hand.
