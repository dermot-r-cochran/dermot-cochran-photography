Nothing in the frame shows it, but this animal was inside a fence and a
National Parks and Wildlife Service ranger was standing by while the picture
was taken. The herd is feral in ancestry — domestic stock, generations back —
and managed in practice, which is a combination the rulebooks have no single
word for. It is enough to rule the photograph out of every nature competition
twice over: they exclude feral animals, and they exclude enclosed ones. Get
close enough for a portrait and the enclosure disappears, which is exactly why
it is worth saying.
