Feral rather than wild, and the difference is only on paper. These are the
descendants of domestic stock left to themselves on an uninhabited island, and
the strictest nature rulebooks — the ones the IPF and FIAP use — count them as
livestock however many generations have passed. A genuinely wild goat, shot
identically, would be eligible for sections this photograph is barred from.
The island has stopped making the distinction; the rules have not.
