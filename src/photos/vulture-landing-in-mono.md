The colour frame has its own page. In monochrome the bird is a shape and
the bush is a texture, and there is nothing else in the frame to look at.
The shape is not clean either: the wing edges are moving at the shutter
speed, and the head sits inside the body's shadow. Colour was carrying the
brown of the secondaries and the pink of the face; taking it out left less,
not more.
