Three moments from one giraffe, thirteen seconds apart at the ends: head down in
the grass, head out sideways, and turned to look at the vehicle. The frames were
aligned on the bushes and laid over the last one, so the grass, the zebra and
the body are a single exposure and only the neck moves. The zebra never looked
up. The grey band along the skyline is Nairobi, through the early haze.
