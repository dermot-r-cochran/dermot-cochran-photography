The window is not a viewpoint. St Begnet's lost its roof long before anyone
thought to build the tower, and the opening frames it only because both happen
to stand on the same small island — roughly eight centuries between them, and
no intention connecting the two. What survives on Dalkey survives by being too
much trouble to remove.
