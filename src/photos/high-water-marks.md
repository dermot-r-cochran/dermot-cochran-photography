July 2026 was the driest July ever recorded in Ireland — 16 mm of rain, about
a sixth of the thirty-year average — with an absolute drought declared across
most of the country. The hosepipe ban covering Dublin and four neighbouring
counties ran from 13 July to 26 August, and was still in force the day this
was taken. The reservoir behind this wall supplies around 35,000 households in
the south of the city.
