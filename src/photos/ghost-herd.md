Six frames of a gazelle group that would not hold still, none of them sharp,
shot at 1/60 on a 400 mm lens from a vehicle. They are aligned on the one thing
in the scene that did not move, the railway that crosses the park on those
piers, and averaged. The viaduct is solid because it stayed put; the gazelles
are transparent because they did not.
