The chick left its nest within a day or two of hatching and will spend the
next seven weeks among these boulders, where the speckled down is camouflage
rather than decoration. A parent stays within a few metres the whole time,
and the reason is not what you might expect: the largest gull in the world
eats the young of every gull on the island, its own neighbours' included, so
a chick this age is safest exactly here. Half an hour later, three of them
