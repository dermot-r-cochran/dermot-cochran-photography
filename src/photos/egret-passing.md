A cattle egret at 1/60 of a second on a 400 mm lens, from a vehicle that was
also moving. Nothing in the frame is sharp and nothing was meant to be kept;
the egret is the only thing travelling in the same direction as the camera,
which is why it is the only thing with a shape. Contrast lifted, nothing else.
