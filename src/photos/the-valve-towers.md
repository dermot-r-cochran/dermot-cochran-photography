The two reservoirs were fed deliberately differently. Water off the stony,
peat-free ground on the sides of the glen was gathered into the upper
reservoir and kept for drinking; the peaty water draining off the bog higher
up went to the lower one, which had only to keep the mills supplied and did
not need to be clear. Two lakes a few hundred metres apart, built at the same
time, holding water of quite different quality on purpose.
