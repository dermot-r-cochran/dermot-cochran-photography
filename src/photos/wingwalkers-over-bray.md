The two figures on the upper wings are not strapped in at the waist. They stand
on a rig braced to the centre section, at roughly 150mph, and the poses are held
deliberately rather than braced against the airflow.

The propellers read as discs rather than blades because the shutter was at
1/250 — slow for a moving subject, and the reason the aircraft look like they
are flying rather than parked in the air.
