Drying Wings

The lake was dug for a castle, and the sea is a few miles off. The bird did not mind. It fished here and came out to this branch, which has been in the water long enough to grow a fern. The sparkle behind is the same lake with the sun on it, a stop or two brighter than the bird. The frames either side of this one are Three Ways to Dry.
