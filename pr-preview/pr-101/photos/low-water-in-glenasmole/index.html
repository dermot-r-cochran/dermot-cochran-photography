Low Water in Glenasmole

The two reservoirs in this valley were built between 1883 and 1887 for the Rathmines Commission, and drinking water was only half the reason. The lower one exists to guarantee a steady flow down the Dodder for the mills strung along it — forty-five of them at the time, grinding flour, making paper and paint and cardboard, tanning, brewing and curing bacon. The mills are long gone. The obligation to keep the river running is what shaped the valley.
