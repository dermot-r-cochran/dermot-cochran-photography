Wild or Cultivated

Wildlife, nature and macro photographs, split by whether the subject was living and growing wild or was planted and tended. A rose from a rose garden and a thistle from a field are both worth photographing; they are not the same kind of picture, and only one of them is eligible for the stricter nature competitions.
