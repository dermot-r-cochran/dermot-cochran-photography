Natural or Built

The same landscape can be three different things. Some of these photographs were made in country that was left to itself, some in the built world, and some — the ones that interest me most — in the ground between, where a reservoir stands in for a lake and a line of groynes has been driven into a beach.

This describes the environment, never the subject standing in it. A balloon over the Mara and a Mustang over Bray are both filed as Natural, because the savannah and the sky are, whatever is crossing them.
